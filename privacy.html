Privacy Policy: Molecule Minute Autopilot

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Molecule Minute Autopilot is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Molecule Minute channel. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

  • YouTube (upload only): to upload and schedule the channel's own videos.
  • Google Drive: to read video scripts from the owner's own queue folder and save the finished video files back to the owner's own Drive.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, sell, or share any personal data.
  • It does not access anyone else's account, and it does not read anything outside the owner's own channel and folders.
  • Sign-in credentials are stored only as encrypted secrets in the owner's private automation settings.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
